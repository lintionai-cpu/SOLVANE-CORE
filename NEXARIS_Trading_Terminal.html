03 · Differs Pro
DIGITDIFF / DIGITOVER · direction + last-digit trigger · 0 cycles
IDLE
Auto-reset & restart cycle when Take-Profit or Stop-Loss is hit
Entry: last tick direction = FALL and last digit == Fall Trigger → buy; direction = RISE and last digit == Rise Trigger → buy. On loss, stake = stake × multiplier (aggressive recovery — use with care). On win, stake resets. "Pro v1" variant trades DIGITOVER with prediction 1; "New Hope" trades DIGITDIFF with prediction 0. Because the multiplier is aggressive, Take-Profit/Stop-Loss are enforced here even though the source XML had none — strongly recommended to keep Auto-Restart's Stop-Loss tight.
