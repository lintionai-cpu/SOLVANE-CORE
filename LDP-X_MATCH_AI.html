Contract Type

Matches mode trades the top-N highest scoring digits simultaneously. Differs mode inverts the logic and trades against the lowest-N scoring digits.

Stake & Exposure

Take-Profit / Stop-Loss (Soft vs Hard Stops)

Take-Profit is a soft stop — cycle P&L resets and auto-restart fires. Stop-Loss / Daily Loss / Max Loss-Streak are hard stops — trading halts and requires manual acknowledgement.

Trade Spacing & Sequencing

Sequential Execution
Wait for each contract to fully settle before firing the next one in the basket
With Sequential Execution ON, the engine buys one digit, waits for that contract to close, then pauses the spacing delay before buying the next digit — so no two contracts are ever open at once. Turn it OFF to fire the whole basket back-to-back (still respecting the spacing delay only, not settlement).

Recovery Engine

Adaptive Recovery (Bounded)
Scales next stake after a loss, capped by multiplier & step limits
Unlimited martingale is prohibited by design — recovery halts and hard-stops after the max step count regardless of confidence.
