About This Platform
DigitAI Signals is an analysis and alerting tool only. It streams live last-digit tick data from Deriv's public WebSocket feed, runs statistical and pattern-based analysis, and surfaces entry signals with confidence scores. It does not place trades, manage stakes, or connect to any trading account. All figures shown are derived from real-time tick data — there is no simulated or demo data. Trading decisions based on these signals are made solely by the user; this tool provides analysis, not financial advice.
