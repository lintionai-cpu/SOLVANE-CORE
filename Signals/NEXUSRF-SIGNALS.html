About NEXUS RF Signals

NEXUS RF Signals connects directly to Deriv's public WebSocket market data feed — no login, no API token, and no account access required.

This app is a pure analysis and alerting tool. It streams live tick data, runs the RF Reversal signal logic, and surfaces clear CALL/PUT entry alerts.

⚠ This app does not place trades, manage an account, or execute orders of any kind. All trading decisions and execution remain entirely with you, on your own Deriv account.
